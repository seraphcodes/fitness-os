Recovery

Muscle is built while you rest.

Sleep

Aim for 7 to 9 hours. Same bedtime within 30 minutes. Phone away 30 minutes before. Dark, cool room. After a night under 6 hours, cut volume rather than skipping.

Highest leverage

Active recovery day

20 to 30 minutes of easy walking, light stretching for hips, chest and lats, optional foam rolling. No gym. Eat your full protein target.

Cardio

Keep it minimal while building. Two easy sessions a week at most, try to avoid it right after legs. Your commute already counts.

Deload

Roughly every 5 to 8 weeks, or when your performance has dropped for two weeks, take one lighter week at about 60% of your usual sets with the same weights. Many people come back stronger.

Stress is physical

University, work and money pressure slow recovery. Three small resets that work: a five minute walk outside after training, ten slow breaths before your first meal, and no phone for the first twenty minutes of the day.

Protocols

A bad day should not become a bad month.

Missed workout

You skipped a planned session

  1. Do not make it up the same day. Rest beats training exhausted.
  2. The next session continues as planned. No punishment sets.
  3. One miss changes nothing. Three in a row means look for the cause.
  4. Tap Not today in Train so your queue stays honest.
  5. Write it in your Sunday review. Patterns show over weeks.

Low budget week

Food money is tight

  1. Eggs: 10 for about 2 to 3 euros is 60g protein and 700 kcal.
  2. Canned tuna or sardines are the cheapest protein per gram.
  3. Oats and rice are cheap, filling calories.
  4. Protect protein first. Drop extras before you drop protein.
  5. Keep taking creatine. One bag lasts 2 to 3 months.

High stress period

Exams, deadlines, money, personal problems

  1. Drop to 3 sessions this week. Minimum effective dose.
  2. Cut sets by 30%, keep the weights.
  3. If you can only do one thing, sleep beats training.
  4. Keep eating. Appetite often drops under stress, and under-eating makes recovery harder.
  5. Keep sessions short and leave the gym feeling better.

No gym access

Travelling, moving, gym closed

  1. Push-ups in three widths cover chest and triceps.
  2. A sturdy table edge or bar works for rows.
  3. Pike push-ups cover shoulders.
  4. Bodyweight squats and lunges maintain legs.
  5. A few missed workouts rarely cost muscle. Weeks of under-eating can. Eat.

Restart after a gap

A week or more off

  1. Week 1 at about 60% of your old volume. Soreness will be loud anyway.
  2. Get food right first: three solid eating days before you worry about training.
  3. Look at your first photo and re-read your goal.
  4. Tell your training partner. Accountability restarts accountability.
  5. Week 2 back to normal. Strength usually comes back faster than it took to build.

My supplement log

Use your product label

This is a log, not a prescription. Write down what is on your product and what you actually take. For vitamin D and zinc, more is not better: ask a doctor or pharmacist, and consider a blood test before taking vitamin D long term. The German Nutrition Society (DGE) uses 20 µg (800 IU) a day as its reference when sunlight is not enough, and the EU upper limit for adults is 100 µg (4000 IU) for vitamin D and 25 mg for zinc.

Skip these

Test boosters, BCAAs, fat burners

Test boosters have little evidence behind them. BCAAs add little when your protein is covered. Take iron only if a blood test shows you need it. Check any pre-workout for caffeine content and your own tolerance.
